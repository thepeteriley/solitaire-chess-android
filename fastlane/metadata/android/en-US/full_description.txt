Play chess with the evaluation in view. Stockfish 19 analyses positions on your device and shows up to five candidate continuations.

Play against an adjustable-strength computer or move both sides yourself. Explore a suggested line, then return to your game. Undo, browse move history, flip the board or load a FEN position.

Portrait puts the board above the analysis. Landscape puts them side by side. Your current game is saved on this device.

Fully offline, with no account, ads, analytics, Google Play Services or network permission. Android distribution: GPLv3 or later; shared interface: MIT.
